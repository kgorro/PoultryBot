## res-cebu-farm-profile | Typical layer farm in Cebu (2019 survey)
Keywords: cebu layer farms, farm profile, typical farm, egg basket, trays per day, farm size, laborers, workers, lohmann, h&n, ready to lay, research, survey, study, cebu, bantayan, argao, dalaguete, pila ka manok, kadako sa farm
Questions:
- What is a typical layer farm in Cebu like?
- How many trays of eggs do Cebu layer farms produce per day?
- Unsa ka dako ang kasagaran nga layer farm sa Cebu?
- Ilang tray ng itlog ang gawa ng karaniwang layer farm sa Cebu?

A 2019 survey of 171 layer farms in nine Cebu towns (mostly the Bantayan Island cluster: Bantayan, Madridejos, Santa Fe) found:
- 67.8% had 15,000 birds or fewer; 90.6% produced 350 trays or fewer per day (51.5% produced 100 trays or fewer).
- 63.2% had four or fewer laborers.
- Stock: Lohmann 66.7%, H&N 14.6%, Asia White 8.2%. 63.7% bought day-old chicks and 36.3% ready-to-lay pullets.
- 63.2% had access to a farm consultant.
- Other animals kept nearby: ruminants 35.1%, dogs and cats 18.7%, pigs 11.7%.
Cebu is called an egg basket, producing an estimated 3 to 4 million eggs a day. These are pre-pandemic figures from a convenience sample, not current province-wide numbers.

## res-cebu-production | Housing, feeding, and lighting on Cebu layer farms
Keywords: cage size, birds per cage, roof type, semi-monitor, welded wire floor, mash, feeding times, beak trimming age, led lights, lighting schedule, deep well, egg collection, culling age, kulungan, hawla, kaon kada adlaw
Questions:
- What cage size do Cebu layer farms use?
- How often do Cebu farms feed and collect eggs?
- Pila ka manok kada hawla sa mga farm sa Cebu?
- Ilang beses magpakain ang mga layer farm sa Cebu?

What surveyed Cebu layer farms reported in 2019:
- Housing: every farm had a dedicated layer house; semi-monitor roofs (75.4%); welded wire floors (46.2%); wood and concrete (39.2%).
- Cages: 15 x 15 inches (37.4%) or 12 x 12 inches (33.3%), most often 2 or 3 birds per cage.
- Feed: mash (78.4%), fed three times a day (49.7%).
- Water: troughs (50.9%), nipple drinkers (22.8%); deep well water (53.2%); always available on 81.9% of farms.
- Brooding: electric brooders (53.2%).
- Beak trimming at 21 days (66.1%); 17.0% did not trim.
- Lighting: LED lamps (66.7%), mostly added in the evening.
- Eggs collected two (38.0%) or three (33.3%) times a day; hens commonly culled at about 24 months (45.6%).
- 52.0% kept no organized records.
These describe common practice, not a recommendation. Follow your strain's management guide.

## res-cebu-biosecurity | Biosecurity gaps on Cebu layer farms
Keywords: biosecurity survey, biosecurity gaps, footbath, fence, visitor logbook, vehicle disinfection, rodent control, tire vat, cleaning schedule, disinfection, cebu biosecurity, research, bisita, sakyanan, ilaga, daga, disinfect
Questions:
- How good is biosecurity on Cebu layer farms?
- What biosecurity do most Cebu poultry farms lack?
- Unsa ang kulang sa biosecurity sa mga farm sa Cebu?
- Ano ang kulang sa biosecurity ng mga poultry farm sa Cebu?

In the 2019 Cebu survey (171 farms), visible measures were common but procedures were rare:
- Fence: 71.3%; footbath: 39.8%
- Vehicle spray: 10.5%; tire vat: 5.8%
- Rodent and pest control: 5.3%
- Visitor logbook: 4.7%
- Equipment cleaning was often done only as needed (32.8%); manure collected daily (26.3%) or every other day (27.5%).
Priorities the authors set for Cebu farms:
- Keep a visitor and vehicle log and disinfect vehicles at the gate.
- Run a rodent and pest control program.
- Clean and disinfect on a fixed schedule, not only when a problem appears.
- Keep farm records for production, health, and medicines.
Ask your municipal agriculture office about biosecurity training.

## res-cebu-health-practices | Vaccination, deworming, and medicine use on Cebu farms
Keywords: booster vaccination, primary vaccines, egg drop syndrome, mycoplasma, marek, deworming as needed, antibiotics hot weather, amoxicillin, doxycycline, albendazole, prudent antibiotic use, antibiotic init, tambal sa init, bakuna booster
Questions:
- What vaccines do Cebu layer farms commonly give?
- Do Cebu farms give antibiotics during hot weather?
- Unsa nga bakuna ang kasagaran ihatag sa mga layer sa Cebu?
- Nagbibigay ba ng antibiotic ang mga farm sa Cebu kapag mainit?

What surveyed Cebu farms reported in 2019:
- Primary vaccines: Newcastle 61.4%, Gumboro 49.7%, fowl pox 49.7%, infectious bronchitis 47.4%, egg drop syndrome 34.5%, Mycoplasma 26.9%, Marek's 24.0%.
- 33.9% gave no booster vaccination.
- Deworming was mostly done as needed (36.8%) or every 3 months (28.7%). Common dewormers named: albendazole, mebendazole, levamisole.
- 79% gave electrolytes, antibiotics, and vitamins during hot weather. Antibiotics most often named in farm history: amoxicillin (67.8%) and doxycycline (53.2%).
The authors recommend booster vaccination planning and prudent antibiotic use. Antibiotics do not treat heat stress: use ventilation, shade, cool clean water, and electrolytes instead, and give antibiotics only for a diagnosed bacterial disease, following the label and the egg withdrawal period. Consult a licensed veterinarian before using any medicine.
