## res-parasite-prevalence | Gut worms in Bantayan layers: how common
Keywords: parasite study, parasite prevalence, worm prevalence, gastrointestinal parasites, fecal exam, bantayan worms, how common worms, porsyento, how many farms, research, survey, ascaridia, heterakis, bulate, bitok, bulati, kagaw sa tiyan
Questions:
- How common are worms in layer farms in Bantayan?
- What did the parasite study in Bantayan find?
- Pila ka farm sa Bantayan ang naay bitok ang manok?
- Gaano karami ang bulate sa mga manok sa Bantayan?

A study of 751 fecal samples from 121 layer farms on Bantayan Island (December 2019, April 2021, June 2021) found gut parasites in 42.6% of samples and on 80.2% of farms.
- Ascaridia (large roundworm): 23.4% of samples
- Heterakis (cecal worm): 11.9%
- Trichostrongylus: 8.3%
- Coccidian oocysts: 6.1%
- Raillietina (tapeworm): 4.1%
- More than one parasite in the same sample: 14.4%
Santa Fe had the highest sample positivity (54.6%), then Bantayan (43.2%) and Madridejos (34.1%). Positivity was highest in April 2021 (50.4%), the dry season.
If you suspect worms, have droppings checked by a veterinarian or laboratory before treating.

## res-parasite-deworming | Deworming on Bantayan layer farms: what the study saw
Keywords: deworming schedule, deworm interval, deworming study, dewormer not working, resistance, fecrt, fecal egg count, purga, pag-purga, ilaga bitok, gamot sa bulate
Questions:
- Does regular deworming prevent worms in Bantayan layers?
- How often do Bantayan farms deworm their layers?
- Kada pila ka bulan mag-purga sa manok sa Bantayan?
- Epektibo ba ang purga sa manok sa Bantayan?

In the Bantayan parasite study, 43.8% of farms reported deworming every 1 to 3 months (records were missing for 42.1%). Farms that dewormed often were not less likely to have positive samples.
This does not prove dewormers fail, but it means deworming alone was not keeping worms down. The authors call for testing whether dewormers still work (fecal egg count reduction tests) before relying on a fixed schedule.
What helps alongside deworming:
- Ask a veterinarian to run a fecal exam before and after treatment.
- Rotate dewormer classes only on veterinary advice, and follow the label for dose and egg withdrawal.
- Clean manure regularly and keep feed and water free of droppings.
Consult a licensed veterinarian or your municipal veterinary office for the dewormer and dose.

## res-parasite-control | Controlling worms and tapeworms on island layer farms
Keywords: prevent worms, tapeworm, cestode, raillietina, beetles, flies, ants, intermediate host, blackhead, histomonas, sanitation, pest control, manure management, likayan bitok, langgam, uk-ok
Questions:
- How do I stop tapeworms in my layers?
- Why does the cecal worm matter for blackhead disease?
- Unsaon paglikay sa bitok sa manok sa farm?
- Paano maiwasan ang tapeworm sa mga manok?

Findings from the Bantayan study and what they mean on the farm:
- Tapeworms (Raillietina) need an intermediate host such as beetles, ants, or flies. Control these with good manure management, dry floors, closed feed storage, and pest control.
- The cecal worm (Heterakis) can carry Histomonas, the cause of blackhead disease, so its presence is a warning sign beyond the worm itself.
- Roundworm eggs survive in manure and litter, so regular manure removal and keeping droppings out of feed and water cut reinfection.
- Wild birds and rodents can bring parasites in: screen houses and control rats.
The authors recommend regular parasite surveillance, sanitation, and manure management on high-density islands like Bantayan. For diagnosis and treatment, consult a licensed veterinarian or your municipal or provincial veterinary office.
