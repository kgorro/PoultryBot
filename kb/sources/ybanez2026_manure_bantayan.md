## res-manure-survey | Bantayan layer manure survey: what farms do
Keywords: manure survey, manure study, bantayan manure, layer manure, manure handling, manure practices, manure removal, tools, shovel, pail, scraper, broom, training, research, study, survey, bantayan, madridejos, santa fe, tae sa manok, ipot, dumi ng manok
Questions:
- How do Bantayan layer farms handle manure?
- What did the study on manure in Bantayan find?
- Unsa ang gibuhat sa mga farm sa Bantayan sa ipot sa manok?
- Paano hinahawakan ng mga layer farm sa Bantayan ang dumi ng manok?

A survey of 136 layer farm owners or managers in Bantayan, Madridejos, and Santa Fe (field visits 2019 to 2023) found:
- Half of the farms had 5,000 layers or fewer. Most used Lohmann stock (76.5%) and bought day-old chicks (68.4%). 90.4% had 1 to 10 workers.
- Manure was removed by hand: shovel and pail (95.6%), broom and dustpan (75.0%), wood scraper with sacks (69.1%). Wheelbarrows and pressure washing were uncommon.
- Most farms produced 1 to 50 sacks of manure per week (80.9%).
- Almost all farms dried manure on the farm before use or sale (95.6%).
- 66.2% reported training on manure handling, most often from the Department of Agriculture (39.7%).
These are baseline figures from a convenience sample, not island-wide estimates.

## res-manure-uses | Where Bantayan layer manure goes and its price
Keywords: sell manure, manure price, price per sack, dried manure, manure buyer, manure market, fertilizer, crops, contractor, presyo ipot, baligya ipot, benta dumi, sako
Questions:
- How much is dried chicken manure sold for per sack in Bantayan?
- Who buys layer manure in Bantayan?
- Pila ang presyo sa ipot sa manok kada sako sa Bantayan?
- Magkano ang benta ng tuyong dumi ng manok bawat sako?

Reported uses of manure on the surveyed Bantayan farms (farms could give more than one):
- Sold as dried manure: 77.9%
- Used on crops after drying: 48.5%
- Collected by other farmers for fertilizer: 45.6%
- Collected by contractors: 34.6%
- Buried or burned on the farm: 16.9%; given to garbage collection: 15.4%
- Wet manure used on crops (8.1%) or sold wet (5.9%) was uncommon.
When manure was sold, it was mostly sold dried (91.2%). The most common price reported was about PHP 5.00 per sack (44.1% of sellers). Prices were pooled over 2019 to 2023, so check current local prices before planning income.

## res-manure-problems | Manure drying and odor problems on Bantayan farms
Keywords: manure drying, hard to dry, wet manure, rainy season, odor, smell, flies, covered storage, drainage, baho, langaw, basa ipot, ulan, mabaho
Questions:
- What are the main manure problems of Bantayan layer farms?
- How can I dry layer manure during the rainy season?
- Lisod mamala ang ipot sa manok kung ting-ulan, unsay buhaton?
- Mabaho ang dumi ng manok sa farm, ano ang dapat gawin?

In the Bantayan survey, difficulty drying manure was the top problem (73.5%), followed by odor (44.1%). Most farms (80.9%) reported no major problem with cleaning itself.
Practical steps the study recommends:
- Protect drying areas from rain with a roof or tarpaulin.
- Use covered storage for dried manure and keep it off the ground.
- Improve drainage around drying and storage areas.
- Handle manure safely: gloves, boots, and a mask when dusty; wash hands after.
- Coordinate pick-up with buyers so dried manure does not sit and get wet again.
Composting, vermicomposting, biochar, and biogas (anaerobic digestion) were suggested only after a local feasibility check, because their cost and benefit on the island were not measured.

## res-manure-knowledge | What farmers know about other uses of manure
Keywords: manure uses, uses of manure, aquaculture, fish pond, cattle feed, fuel, nutrients, awareness, knowledge, gamit sa ipot, unsa magamit, gamit ng dumi
Questions:
- What other uses of chicken manure do Bantayan farmers know about?
- Can layer manure be used for fish ponds or fuel?
- Unsa pa ang gamit sa ipot sa manok gawas sa abono?
- Ano pa ang ibang gamit ng dumi ng manok?

When shown a list, surveyed Bantayan farmers recognized these uses of chicken manure: saleable product (63.2%), fertilizer (55.9%), source of nutrients (55.1%), aquaculture (34.6%), cattle feed (25.0%), fuel (25.0%), and fish pond use (6.6%). 58.8% said they were not aware of other uses.
Fertilizer and sale are the established uses. Feeding manure to animals or putting it in fish ponds carries disease and water-quality risks, so ask your municipal agriculture office or BFAR before trying these.
