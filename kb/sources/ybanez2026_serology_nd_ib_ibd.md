## res-serology-results | Antibody levels for ND, IB, and Gumboro in Bantayan layers
Keywords: serology, antibody, antibodies, blood test, titer, immunity, immunocomb, serosurvey, protection level, newcastle antibody, gumboro antibody, ibd antibody, ib antibody, research, study, bantayan, resistensya, proteksyon
Questions:
- What did the blood test study of Bantayan layers show for Newcastle, IB, and Gumboro?
- Are Bantayan layers protected against Gumboro?
- Unsa ang resulta sa blood test sa manok sa Bantayan para sa Newcastle ug Gumboro?
- May sapat bang proteksyon ang mga layer sa Bantayan laban sa Gumboro?

Blood from 524 layers on 55 farms in Bantayan and Madridejos was tested with the ImmunoComb kit.
- Birds with detectable antibodies: Newcastle disease 99.0%, infectious bronchitis 99.4%, Gumboro (IBD) 97.5%.
- Birds in the kit's adequate antibody category: Newcastle 97.5%, IB 92.2%, Gumboro only 62.4%.
- For Gumboro, 35.1% of birds had antibodies below the adequate level.
So most birds had met these viruses or vaccines, but Gumboro antibody levels were the weakest. The kit category is not proof of protection, and the study did not test for active infection.
Ask your veterinarian about serological monitoring for your own flock. For diagnosis and treatment, consult a licensed veterinarian or your municipal or provincial veterinary office.

## res-serology-vaccination | Vaccination records and gaps on Bantayan layer farms
Keywords: vaccination records, vaccine history, ibd vaccination, gumboro vaccination, booster, vaccine schedule, serosurveillance, vaccine failure, hatchery vaccination, bakuna, rekord sa bakuna, talaan ng bakuna
Questions:
- How many Bantayan farms vaccinate against Gumboro?
- Why do my layers have Gumboro antibodies if I never vaccinated?
- Pila ka farm sa Bantayan ang nagbakuna og Gumboro?
- Bakit may antibody sa Gumboro ang manok kahit hindi binakunahan?

Among 55 Bantayan Island farms, reported vaccination was: Newcastle disease 45 farms (81.8%), infectious bronchitis 32 (58.2%), Gumboro 8 (14.5%). Yet 97.5% of birds had Gumboro antibodies.
Likely reasons: chicks or ready-to-lay pullets were vaccinated at the hatchery or grower before arrival, or birds were exposed to the field virus. Most farms could not say the vaccine brand, strain, route, or booster sequence.
What the authors recommend:
- Keep complete vaccine records, including what the hatchery or pullet supplier gave.
- Review how vaccines are stored and delivered (cold chain, water quality, timing).
- Do routine antibody monitoring and strengthen biosecurity.
- Investigate which virus strains circulate on the island.
Vaccines and schedules should be planned with a licensed veterinarian; follow the product label.

## res-serology-newcastle-risk | Newcastle risk even in vaccinated flocks
Keywords: newcastle vaccinated still sick, vaccinated flock outbreak, genotype vii, velogenic, high density island, newcastle risk, vaccine not working, nabakunahan pero namatay, nabakunahan na pero nagkasakit
Questions:
- Can a vaccinated layer flock still get Newcastle disease?
- If most Bantayan birds have Newcastle antibodies, is there still a risk?
- Nabakunahan na ang manok, pwede pa ba ma-Newcastle?
- Bakuna na ang manok pero namamatay pa rin, bakit?

Most Bantayan layers tested had adequate Newcastle antibodies (97.5%), but that does not mean there is no risk. Philippine studies have found virulent genotype VII Newcastle virus causing deaths on vaccinated commercial layer farms.
On a crowded island, heat stress, transport, stocking density, feed quality (including mold toxins), and poor vaccine handling can weaken vaccine response.
If birds die suddenly or show twisted necks, green diarrhea, or breathing problems: stop moving birds and eggs, isolate the house, and report to the municipal or provincial veterinary office right away. Newcastle disease is reportable.
